Watford FC - Club Shop

For the opening of Watford FC’s new club shop in the Harlequin Shopping Centre, I was responsible for creating vinyl graphics across the shop’s walls and windows. The designs were developed to bring the club’s identity into the retail space while working within the dimensions and requirements of the new store.

I worked closely with the Head of Retail to confirm measurements and sizing across each area, adapting the artwork accordingly before preparing and supplying the final designs as print-ready artwork to the printers. This involved ensuring all files were correctly set up and ready for production and installation.
